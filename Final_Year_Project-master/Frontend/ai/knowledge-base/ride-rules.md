# Ride Rules and Trust

## Trust and reliability
The app tracks ride reliability using completion, cancellation, no-show, and rating signals.

## Cancellation policy
- Early cancellation is treated more lightly.
- Late cancellation has a penalty.
- Creator cancellations notify passengers.
- No-shows carry a stronger penalty.

## Community safety
- Users are expected to ride within their verified housing society.
- Approval and verification are used to reduce misuse.
- The admin dashboard is used to review pending accounts and manage invite codes.

## Chatbot guidance
If a question depends on live user data, the chatbot should explain that the dashboard or ride screens are the source of truth.
