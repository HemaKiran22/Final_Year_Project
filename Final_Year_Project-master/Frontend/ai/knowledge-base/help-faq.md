# Help and FAQ

## How do I join the app?
You sign up with your account details and invite/approval flow. If the account is pending, an admin must approve it before full access is granted.

## How do I find a ride?
Open the dashboard and search by destination, date, or time. The app shows matching open rides.

## How do I post a ride?
Use the ride posting flow in the dashboard. Enter destination, date, time, seats, and price.

## What is the chatbot for?
The chatbot explains the app, policies, and project information. It should be used for guidance, not as the place to directly perform ride actions.

## Where can I see my rides?
Open the dashboard and go to My Rides.

## What if I need support?
Use the Help & Support screen or ask the chatbot for the relevant app policy or feature explanation.
